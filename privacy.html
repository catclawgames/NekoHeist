Who we are

Neko Heist is made by [your name or studio]. Contact: [email address].

What we collect

  • An account ID. When you first play online you get an anonymous guest account (a random ID). If you choose to link Google or Apple, we receive the email address and name that provider shares.
  • Game data. Your progress (coins, rank, unlocked cats and traps), your base layout, the player name you choose, and records of heists on other players' bases, including replays of those heists.
  • Device storage. The game saves progress and settings on your device.

We do not show ads and do not sell your data.

How it is used

To run the game: saving your progress across devices, letting other players raid your base, and showing you who raided you. Your base layout, player name and rank are visible to other players.

Where it is stored

Online data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). See Firebase's privacy information.

Deleting your data

Open Profile in the game and tap Delete my account and data. This removes your account, cloud save, base and the raid records on your base. You can also email [email address] to ask for deletion.
